Jesus announces that God’s kingdom is at hand, and the disciple responds by joining its movement through acts of love, service, generosity, welcome and witness. Seeds are often small and easily overlooked, but they carry the possibility of life beyond their apparent size. Mission is therefore not reserved for specialists or confined to church programmes.

The Park Paradise festival offered a concrete picture of this kind of mission. Play, Grow and Create gave people different ways to encounter welcome and community: games and energy, care for the natural world, art, storytelling, music, prayer and shared food. The point was not to disguise a religious message inside an event, but to practise the generous, hospitable and creative life of God’s kingdom in public.

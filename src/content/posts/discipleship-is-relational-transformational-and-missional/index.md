Discipleship is not simply learning Christian information or adopting a moral code. It is an apprenticeship to Jesus: learning from him how to lead our lives as he would lead them if he were us. That makes discipleship relational, because it begins in friendship with Jesus; transformational, because the Spirit reshapes our character; and missional, because disciples join the work of God’s kingdom in ordinary life.

The five icons—Cross, Fire, Table, Vine and Seeds—are not a complete system or replacement for the creeds. They are memorable doorways into the life of following Jesus. Each image invites biblical reflection, personal response and a practice that can be shared in a community.

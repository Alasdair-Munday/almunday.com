The *imago Dei* is borne by the whole of humanity, including our interdependence. We do not image God most fully as autonomous individuals but as people who depend upon others and show up for those who depend upon us.

Humanity therefore needs its full diversity—including every form of ability and diffability—to represent God to creation. Difference is not a departure from a supposedly ideal human form. Each person contributes something necessary to humanity’s shared vocation of making God visible in the world.

There is no room for human hierarchy in this account of the *imago Dei*. No degree of independence, productivity, intelligence, strength or social status makes one person a fuller image of God than another. The image is not possessed competitively; it is borne together.

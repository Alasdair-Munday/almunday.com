The confession that Jesus is Lord is the foundation for Michael Rhodes’ reimagining of biblical politics. If Jesus is Lord, public life, church identity and Christian ideas of power must be reordered around his reign rather than around the political powers and identities that compete for our allegiance.

This is not a marginal theological claim. It is the centre of a biblical politics that asks what faithful discipleship looks like in public, communal life.

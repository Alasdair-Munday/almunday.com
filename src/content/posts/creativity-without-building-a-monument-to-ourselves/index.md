**The Vision:** The culmination of God’s story is not a pristine garden without human history, but a glorious city filled with the refined cultural treasures, arts, and labors of humanity.

**The Problem:** We often view our daily work, art, and creative endeavors as having no eternal value, or we idolize them for earthly recognition and applause.

**The Gospel Freedom:** Because our worth is settled at the Cross, our creativity is redeemed. We are free to "sub-create"—using our gifts not to build a monument to ourselves (like Babel), but to honor God and love our neighbor.

**The Spirit’s Empowerment:** The Spirit equips us with skills, ingenuity, and imagination to shape culture, knowing that good, true, and beautiful work done in Christ is not in vain (1 Corinthians 15:58)

**Passages to explore:** Revelation 21:24–26; Genesis 2:18–20; Exodus 31:1–6; 1 Corinthians 15:58.

## Question to develop

What gifts or “talents” do you have and are you using them? What would it look like for you to steward your creativity well?

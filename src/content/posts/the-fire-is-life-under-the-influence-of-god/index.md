The fire image describes discipleship as life in the company of the Holy Spirit. The Spirit reminds disciples whose they are, refines them into the likeness of Jesus, guides them, gives gifts for the common good and grows fruit in their character. Christians cannot become disciples through willpower alone; they must keep receiving and responding to God’s presence.

This makes spiritual formation a practice of attention and surrender. The disciple learns to create space for prayer, silence, Scripture, confession and discernment, while also testing perceived guidance in community. Being filled with the Spirit is not about chasing intensity for its own sake. It is about becoming available to God’s direction and allowing love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control to become visible.

The cross is not only the place where Christians explain forgiveness; it is the image that gathers the whole shape of discipleship. At the cross, shame is answered by love, sin loses its power, death is defeated and the character of God is disclosed. The cross shows what God is like and how passionately God holds creation.

Yet the cross also speaks about the disciple. To follow Jesus is to receive a new life and to surrender the old one, taking up the cross daily. The result is not grim self-erasure but a life of trust, gratitude, obedience and freedom. The cross therefore holds together costly love received and costly love practised.

Purity is not morality. Leviticus distinguishes ritual states from moral wrongdoing, so being described as “unclean” does not automatically mean that someone has sinned. Ritual impurity is closely connected with mortality, bodily vulnerability and the forces and signs of death.

This clarifies Jesus’ encounters with impurity. When he touches the sick, the bleeding and the dead, impurity does not overcome him. Life moves in the other direction: Jesus confronts the forces of death and restores people to life, community and worship.

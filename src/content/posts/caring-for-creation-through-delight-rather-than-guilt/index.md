**The Vision:** God's redemptive plan includes the renewal of the entire physical cosmos. The river flowing from God's presence brings flourishing, abundance, and life to the barren and dead places.

**The Problem:** We often swing between two extremes: treating the material world as disposable (escapism) or feeling paralyzed by despair and environmental guilt.

**The Gospel Freedom:** In Christ, God is reconciling all things to Himself, both on earth and in heaven (Colossians 1:20). Creation is not headed for total annihilation, but for resurrection and release from decay (Romans 8:21).

**The Spirit’s Empowerment:** The Spirit invites us to share in the Father’s delight over creation, motivating practical, joyful stewardship rather than fear-driven anxiety.

**Passages to explore:** Ezekiel 47:9; Genesis 1:24–31; Genesis 2:10–17; Romans 8:19–28.

## Question to develop

What is the difference between caring for creation out of "guilt" versus caring for it as an act of Christian worship and hope?

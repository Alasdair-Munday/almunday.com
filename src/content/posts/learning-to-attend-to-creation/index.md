The Spirit invites us to share in the Father’s delight over creation, motivating practical, joyful stewardship rather than fear-driven anxiety.

## Look at the birds; consider the flowers

**Passage to explore:** Matthew 6:25–34.

What is Jesus calling us to do in this passage?

How can we practice this in our life?

## Food for thought

1. How has spending intentional time outdoors or paying close attention to nature shaped your view of God's character?
2. What is the difference between caring for creation out of "guilt" versus caring for it as an act of Christian worship and hope?
3. What is one tangible, everyday habit you can adopt this week to enjoy and care for God's physical world?

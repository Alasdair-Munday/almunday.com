## The deep-cut Hebrew Bible reference in Romans’ famous verse that transforms how we understand the gospel

Paul’s declaration that he is “not ashamed of the gospel” and that “the righteous will live by faith” draws Romans into the argument of Habakkuk.

Habakkuk is concerned with the law’s apparent ineffectiveness in restraining sin and violence, and questions God’s faithfulness to his promise to Israel. Paul’s use of Habakkuk therefore frames the gospel as God’s answer to the intertwined problem of Israel, the nations, sin and divine faithfulness. Reading the quotation in its prophetic context can transform what we think Paul means by “the gospel.”

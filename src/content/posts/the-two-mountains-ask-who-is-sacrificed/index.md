The two mountains offer a compact biblical diagnostic: do the strong suffer for the sake of the weak, or do the weak suffer for the sake of the strong?

On God’s mountain, life and authority are received as gifts and abundance flows outward. Power moves towards vulnerability. The one with greatest authority becomes the servant of all, and the Lamb gives his life for others.

On the counterfeit mountain, humans seize security and life. Power and abundance are concentrated while outsiders, enemies and vulnerable people bear the cost. The weak are sacrificed to preserve the position of the strong.

The question is therefore not only what a vision promises, but who must suffer to bring it about and who benefits from that suffering.

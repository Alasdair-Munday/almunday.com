The two mountains offer a compact biblical diagnostic. On God’s mountain, life and authority are received and abundance flows outward. On the counterfeit mountain, humans seize security and life, concentrating power while outsiders bear the cost. One way to tell them apart is to ask: who has to be sacrificed to maintain this vision?

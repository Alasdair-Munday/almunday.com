Revelation destabilises the assumption that victory means becoming a stronger Caesar. John hears of a conquering Lion and sees a Lamb standing as though slaughtered. Christlike power is power for and with: self-giving, faithful witness, solidarity, liberation, and life offered for others—not preservation of the self through the sacrifice of others.

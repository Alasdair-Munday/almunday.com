In the parable of the ten virgins, wisdom is not merely intellectual understanding or private preparation for a future crisis. The lamps evoke the vocation of God’s people to be light to the world.

The wise are ready to fulfil that destiny both at the moment of fulfilment and throughout the long period of waiting. Readiness for Christ’s return is not passive anticipation or an attempt to escape the present. It means keeping the light burning by faithfully inhabiting our vocation now.

Eschatological wisdom therefore joins present faithfulness to future hope: becoming light to the world as we wait, so that we are ready to welcome Christ when he comes.

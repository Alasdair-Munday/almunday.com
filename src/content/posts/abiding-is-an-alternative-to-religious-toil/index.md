“The task is not to toil but to tarry” was the title of my sermon on Jesus as the true vine in John 15. This outline is a starting point for expanding the sermon into a full essay.

## Essay skeleton

- **Jesus as the true vine — John 15:** remaining in him is the source of fruitfulness.
- **The wider biblical story:** Eden’s tree → Israel as vine → Christ the true vine → Revelation’s tree of life.
- **“The task is not to toil but to tarry”:** sonship versus slavery; belonging rather than anxious striving.
- **What abiding feels like:** the Covid “open line” illustration—an ongoing awareness of another’s presence.
- **Why abiding needs cultivating:** the attention economy competes for our awareness.
- **Three roots of abiding:** Scripture, church and stillness.
- **Contemplation:** develop the baby illustration here. Retrieve the original sermon notes for the details.
- **Abiding together:** the implications for the church as a community.
- **Conclusion:** return to the refrain, with fruitfulness flowing from abiding.

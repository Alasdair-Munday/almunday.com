Almost all the major characters in the Old Testament are so deeply flawed that, in almost any other narrative context, we would recognise many of them as villains. They deceive, exploit, abuse power, commit violence and sacrifice others to protect their own position.

Familiarity and religious framing can make readers treat these figures as uncomplicated heroes, but the biblical narratives preserve their failures and moral compromises rather than concealing them. Being chosen, empowered or used within God’s purposes does not make every action of a biblical leader legitimate.

This is not simply a collection of exemplary lives to imitate. It is a story of God remaining faithful while working through people who repeatedly misunderstand, distort and resist the good purposes in which they have been invited to participate.

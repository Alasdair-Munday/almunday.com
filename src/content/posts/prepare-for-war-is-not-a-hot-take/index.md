The apocalyptic strand throughout Scripture demands that Christians always prepare for war. This is not a novel prophecy tied to one moment, but the continual posture of a people who know that every earthly power can be shaken. As the author of Hebrews teaches, the only kingdom that cannot be shaken is the kingdom of God.

Preparation therefore means learning to remain faithful when rival kingdoms demand our allegiance, confident that God’s kingdom alone will endure.

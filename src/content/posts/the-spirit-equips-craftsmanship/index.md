The Spirit equips us with skills, ingenuity, and imagination to shape culture, knowing that good, true, and beautiful work done in Christ is not in vain (1 Corinthians 15:58)

## Bezalel and Oholiab

**Passage to explore:** Exodus 31:1–6.

Do you see your talents as a gift empowered by the Spirit of God?

## Questions to develop

What gifts or “talents” do you have and are you using them? What would it look like for you to steward your creativity well?

What work, craftsmanship, or creative outlet do you feel particularly drawn to in your current season of life?

What is one creative way you can use your skills to bless your local community or neighborhood this month?

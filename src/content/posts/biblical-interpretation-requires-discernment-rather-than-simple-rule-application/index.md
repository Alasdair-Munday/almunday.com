Biblical law is first and foremost wisdom literature. It forms a people capable of discerning faithful action in particular circumstances, rather than supplying simple rules for mechanical application.

This connects with John Walton’s *The Lost World of the Torah* and the ethics chapter in Helen Paynter’s *Blessed Are the Peacemakers*. The question is not merely, “What rule applies?” but, “What kind of wise, faithful and peaceable people is this instruction forming us to become?”
